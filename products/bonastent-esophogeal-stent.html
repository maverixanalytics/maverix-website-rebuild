Features

Bonastent® Esophageal Stents are among the most technologically advanced non-vascular, self-expandable metallic stents available today. A patented nitinol hook-and-cross wire structure enables anatomical adaptation, reduced migration, and decreased tumor in-growth, while pre-loaded delivery devices enhance ease of placement.

  • Low shortening rate — precise positioning
  • Inner silicone covering — reduces in-growth and migration
  • Optimal radial force and returning capability — patient conformability
  • Segmental compression design via hook-and-cross wire structure
  • Reduced delivery device diameter
  • Recapture capability up to 70% deployment
  • Latex-free — MR Conditional
