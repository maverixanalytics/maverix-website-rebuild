A single-use, console-free cryobiopsy probe that mounts to the bronchoscope, intended for palliative devitalization (destruction) of tissue during interventional procedures by the application of extreme cold and cryo-adhesion, for applications such as the removal of foreign bodies, mucus plugs, blood clots, necrotic tissue, tissue tumors (palliative recanalization) and tissue biopsies. It is handheld and self-contained, powered by the same single-use Cryo cartridge as the Narwhal probe, with no cryo console to purchase, service or wheel into the room.
