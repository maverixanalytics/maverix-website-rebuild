Maverix Medical devices are prescription-only and intended for use by trained physicians. Complete indications, contraindications, warnings, precautions, and directions for use are provided in each product’s Instructions for Use (IFU).

## Electronic Instructions for Use (eIFU)

Instructions for Use are available electronically. Printed copies are available at no additional cost on request — contact Customer Care and we will send one within seven calendar days.

## Requesting a Printed Copy

Email customercare@thoracent.com or call (888) 978-0232, Monday–Friday, 8:30–5:00 ET. Please include the product name, catalog number, and lot number shown on the device label.

## Reporting an Adverse Event

To report a suspected adverse event or product complaint, contact customercare@thoracent.com. In the United States, adverse events may also be reported to the FDA through <a href="https://www.fda.gov/safety/medwatch-fda-safety-information-and-adverse-event-reporting-program" rel="noopener" target="_blank">MedWatch</a>.
